This project runs two independent implementations of I2P (the Java reference router and i2pd, written in C++) as two separate vantage points on the network. Each builds its own local netDb purely through organic peer gossip. Comparing what each one has actually observed answers a real question: do different implementations see the same slice of the network, or meaningfully different ones? A single vantage point can't answer this at all -- you'd have no way to know whether what you're measuring reflects the real network or just that one router's blind spots.

Overlap Trend Over Time

Both routers may have initially bootstrapped from overlapping reseed servers, which could inflate apparent overlap in the first day or two before organic gossip diverges their views. If this trends downward over the collection window, that's a real, citable finding about how quickly the two implementations' views diverge -- not noise.
