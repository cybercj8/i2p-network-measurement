"Movement" here means a router's IP-derived geolocation changed between observations -- not physical relocation. For a router to show up here, MaxMind resolved its IP to a meaningfully different location across pipeline runs, which most plausibly means the underlying IP address itself changed (VPN, rotating-IP hosting, dynamic residential IP, etc.), not that server hardware moved. Distance is the sum of great-circle hops between consecutive observations, not a straight line start-to-end -- a router that bounces back and forth between two cities racks up distance every hop, not just once.
